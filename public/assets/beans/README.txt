How to add custom images:

1. Browse to this folder: `cafe-bean-tracker/public/assets/beans/`
2. Save your image files here (JPG or PNG).
3. Name the files to match the Bean ID.

Examples:
- Starbucks House Blend -> `sb-house.jpg`
- Tullys House Blend -> `tc-house.jpg`
- Kaldi Mild Kaldi -> `k-mild.jpg`

The app will automatically display these images if they exist.
